## The lines

**Slogan**

> **Your list. Taken care of.**

**Supporting line** (always directly under the slogan when there is room)

> **The whole shop, one honest total, nothing bought until you say yes.**

**Positioning statement** (internal; the test for every feature and message)

> For the person who keeps the household fed, Trollie is a shopping helper that remembers what your family buys, puts the order together, shows one honest total including delivery, and buys only when you say yes. Unlike a single store's app, it works for your household, not the shop.

The supporting line replaces "AI-powered shopping, on your terms", which is retired.

---

## The three promises (always in this order)

1. **Less to remember.** Your usual items, your brands, your regular meals. You stop being the family's memory.
2. **One honest total.** Shop prices, delivery and our fee are all shown before you decide. When we find a saving, we show it. When there isn't one, we say so.
3. **The shop gets done.** We place the order, follow it through, and sort out problems and refunds. We only ever buy with your yes.

---

## What "honest total" commits us to

These are product rules as well as marketing claims. Marketing may not promise more than the product does.

- **Shop prices.** No markup on items, and the same price for everyone. We never charge one person more than another for the same item.
- **One visible fee**, shown before approval. The fee model is still being tested (fixed per order, a capped percentage, or an optional subscription), but it is always visible.
- **Savings measured against a stated baseline:** the same items at one shop, delivery included. We never count benefits the customer can't actually use, and never count a subscription they already pay for as a saving.
- **No saving is still shown.** If the saving is smaller than our fee, the customer is told before approving.
- **No surprise swaps.** Your brands stay your brands. Any change means a fresh approval.

## Words

| Use | Avoid in headlines and first screens | Why |
|---|---|---|
| shopping helper, Trollie | AI agent, autonomous, bot | Most South Africans aren't familiar with "AI". "Agent" belongs in the advanced, connect-your-own-agent section |
| your list, your usual shop, your brands | basket optimisation, optimiser | Plain words |
| one honest total, everything shown up front | cheapest, lowest price guaranteed | We only claim savings we can show |
| you say yes, you approve | passkey, mandate, cryptographic | Say what it does; explain how only when asked |
| the shops near you, participating shops | every shop, all retailers | Only claim the shops that are actually live |
| money set aside for groceries, a grocery budget | wallet, envelope, grant | "Wallet" may be used inside the app; not in a headline |
| we sort out problems and refunds | reconciliation, compensation | Plain words |

**While anything is simulated,** label it SIMULATED — NOT REAL MONEY. Never describe a simulated capability as live.

## Order of the story (decks, proposals, website)

1. The household's problem: remembering, planning, comparing, chasing.
2. What Trollie does about it: the three promises.
3. One customer's order, start to finish.
4. Why it can be trusted: approval, honest total, refunds.
5. *Then* the market context (AI agents, payments) and the technology and security.
