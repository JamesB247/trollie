# Trollie brand

Use these exact values for the website, the platform's pages and presentation templates.
Assets are in `docs/brand/`; the app's pages use `app/static/brand/favicon_64.png`.

## Slogan

> **Your list. Taken care of.**
> The whole shop, one honest total, nothing bought until you say yes.

Locked on 29 October 2026. Wording, the three promises and words to avoid are in [`POSITIONING.md`](POSITIONING.md). The earlier line "AI-powered shopping, on your terms" is retired.

## Colours

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Evergreen | `#123D32` | Primary brand colour: logo, headings, navigation and main buttons |
| Warm cream | `#F6F3E9` | Main background for the website, banners and presentation slides |
| Golden yellow | `#F4C95D` | Signature accent: the logo's dot, selected highlights and small promotional elements |
| Soft sage | `#DDE8DA` | Secondary panels, supporting illustrations and subtle section backgrounds |
| Deep ink | `#172B24` | Body text, product information, prices and form labels |

## How to apply it

- Let cream dominate: roughly 70% cream, 20% evergreen and 10% accents.
- Primary actions, such as approving a basket, use evergreen buttons with cream text.
- Highlights on yellow or sage use dark text.
- Keep prices and order details calm: ink text, consistent spacing, restrained accents.
- Carry the trolley symbol through the product: app icon, avatar and a small stamp on receipts.
- The simulated-money banner (`SIMULATED — NOT REAL MONEY`) stays on every page that shows
  money; it uses golden yellow with ink text.
